Medical Claim Annotation

Fact-checking claims from medical answers

You are helping to validate an automatic labelling pipeline for medical question answering in Persian. An AI model was asked “tell me everything you know about <medical term>”. Its answer was split into short factual claims. For each claim shown, we need your independent expert judgement on whether it is medically correct. Your labels will be compared against the automatic labels and against other experts.

What you will see

  • Left: the question and the model's full answer. The sentence the claim was taken from is highlighted.
  • Right: the claim, then the questions you answer about it.

Question 1 — Is the claim medically correct?

LabelUse it when
Correct (درست) Everything the claim states is supported by standard, current medical references (e.g. standard anatomy / physiology / pathology / pharmacology textbooks, clinical guidelines).
Incorrect (نادرست) The claim contains any factual error, or contradicts established knowledge, or attributes something to the wrong structure / disease / drug. A claim that is mostly right but contains one wrong detail is Incorrect.
Can't decide (نامشخص) You cannot determine correctness: the claim is too vague to check, is not a factual statement, is a fragment, or is outside what you can verify even after looking it up. Do not use this as a way to avoid effort — look it up first.
  • Judge the claim, not the whole answer. Do not let other (right or wrong) parts of the answer influence you.
  • Judge it as an answer to the question asked. The question is always about one medical term; a true statement about a different term is Incorrect.
  • You may use textbooks, guidelines and search engines. Please do not use AI chatbots to decide the label — that would defeat the purpose of the validation.
  • You are not shown the automatic label on purpose. Please do not try to guess it.

Question 2 — Is the claim a faithful restatement of the highlighted sentence? (quick)

  • Yes: the claim says (part of) what the highlighted sentence says, without adding or changing facts.
  • No: the claim adds information that is not in the sentence, changes its meaning, or is unrelated.

This is separate from correctness: a faithful claim can be false, and an unfaithful claim can be true.

Practicalities

  • Your progress is saved automatically in this browser. Use Export my annotations when you finish (or any time as a backup) and send the downloaded .json file back. Use Import progress to continue on another computer.
  • Keyboard: 1 Correct · 2 Incorrect · 3 Can't decide · Y/N faithful · ←/→ previous/next.
  • Please annotate alone, without discussing items with other annotators. Use the note box for anything unclear.
خلاصه به فارسی
  • به هر پرسش، یک مدل هوش مصنوعی پاسخ داده و پاسخ به «ادعاهای» کوتاه تقسیم شده است. جملهٔ مربوط به ادعا در پاسخ هایلایت شده است.
  • درست: تمام مطالب ادعا مطابق منابع معتبر و به‌روز پزشکی است.
  • نادرست: ادعا حتی یک اشتباه واقعی دارد (حتی اگر بیشتر آن درست باشد) یا به ساختار/بیماری/دارویی دیگر مربوط است.
  • نامشخص: ادعا مبهم، ناقص یا غیرقابل راستی‌آزمایی است و پس از جست‌وجوی منابع هم نمی‌توان دربارهٔ آن تصمیم گرفت.
  • فقط خودِ ادعا را قضاوت کنید، نه کل پاسخ را. لطفاً برای تعیین برچسب از چت‌بات‌های هوش مصنوعی استفاده نکنید و با سایر ارزیاب‌ها مشورت نکنید.
  • پرسش دوم: آیا ادعا بازنویسی وفادارانه‌ای از جملهٔ هایلایت‌شده است (بدون افزودن یا تغییر اطلاعات)؟
